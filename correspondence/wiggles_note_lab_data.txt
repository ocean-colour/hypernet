Dear Joel and Kevin,

Thank you, Joel, for the HYPSTAR lab wavelength-calibration files.  They
were exactly what we needed, and a first pass already tells us a lot.

What the lab data show

1. They confirm the two instrument classes we found in the field data.
   The lab FWHM of L minus E, over the 405-546 nm lamp lines, is +0.39 to
   +0.47 nm on 120242, 122302 and 122304, and -0.06 to +0.02 nm on
   121222, 121231, 122303 and 122305.  These agree with our Fraunhofer-
   line fits to the Release 2 spectra.  122303, which we had as
   borderline, is clearly in the E = L group.

2. Our field FWHM models agree with the lamp-line widths to within about
   0.1 nm, channel by channel.

3. On the narrow-E instruments, the E line profile is flat-topped
   (super-Gaussian exponent 2.5 to 2.9), while L is Gaussian on every
   instrument.  We will include this shape in our correction.

4. From the polynomials we can reconstruct each instrument's E and L
   wavelength grids.  Cropped to 350-1100 nm, they reproduce the
   processor's grids exactly.  Until now we had to use the 122304 E grid
   as a stand-in for the other instruments.

5. All the reference lines are air wavelengths, which answers our
   earlier question about the scale.

6. The 4th-order polynomial misses the same lines by the same amount on
   every instrument and both channels: about +0.09 nm at Hg 404.7 nm,
   -0.09 nm at Hg 546.1 nm, and about 0.1 nm in the NIR.  This may explain
   part of a small common wavelength pattern we see in the field data.
   We will look into it and report back.

Questions

1. The processor still uses the 2023_01 wavelength polynomials for 122304
   after its November 2024 recalibration, and likewise for 122302.  The
   2024_09 lab polynomials differ by 0.02-0.05 nm rms, and by up to 0.10 nm.
   Is keeping the older wavelengths intentional?

2. Is there a lab wavelength calibration for 121222 from its March 2025
   recalibration?  The zip has only the 2021_07 epochs for that unit.

3. What is the wl column in lamp_vnir.dat?  It looks like a fixed nominal
   grid for each instrument, perhaps the factory one, and deltawl is
   measured against it.

4. How does the lamp illuminate the irradiance entrance optic during the
   wavelength calibration?  Is it into the diffuser, at what angle, and
   does it fill it?  In the lab the E profile is flat-topped (exponent about
   2.8), while our solar-line fit on the VEIT sequence gives about 2.2.  We
   would like to know whether sunlight and skylight fill the diffuser
   differently from the lamp.

5. Is there any news on the L1A delivery (L1A_IRR, L1A_RAD, L1C_ALL and
   L2A_REF for the sequences in our request) and the RAD and IRR
   calibration files?  The remaining checks, and the before-and-after test
   on real data, depend on them.
